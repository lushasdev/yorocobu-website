Joy is the guide for this site. Ask her anything Yorocobu has published and she
will point you to it.

The name is the English of 喜ぶ, *to be glad* — so the guide and the company are
the same word in two languages, and 喜 is the mark the site opens on.

She is not a person, and she will say so if you ask. She answers from a small set
of published entries about Yorocobu and nothing else, which is why she will tell
you plainly when something is not in them rather than guessing. When that
happens she can pass the question to Ethan.
